Outside of research, I enjoy playing badminton.

Research

My work centers on robot learning: helping robots acquire precise, generalizable manipulation skills by combining scalable data collection with knowledge from pretrained foundation models. To this end, my research includes:

  • Scalable data collection for dexterous manipulation, through teleoperation and human-in-the-loop pipelines;
  • Foundation models for manipulation, using VLMs for precise, self-correcting agentic control;
  • Real-to-sim reconstruction and physical property estimation for model predictive control.

Projects (* indicates equal contribution)

Self-Supervised Precise Agentic Manipulation Ongoing

Project Lead · Advised by Prof. Erdem Bıyık and Prof. Jiajun Wu

Stanford SVL & USC LiraLab · Aug 2026 – Present

An agentic, VLM-based pipeline for precise manipulation that learns from real interaction observations and actively corrects its actions when keypoint or target estimates are inaccurate. It reaches ~80% success on the ManiSkill PlugCharger task, compared with ~60% for the baseline.

Probe-Guided Optimization for Object Hallucination Mitigation in Large Vision-Language Models

Zhangyu Wang*, Qinling Hou*

CSCI 444 Course Project · Spring 2026 / Report (PDF)

Linear probes trained on synthetic CLEVR-MATE data detect cross-modal entity binding errors. Probe-guided hidden-state interventions improve VLM answers without retraining the base models.

Zero-Shot 3D Model Interior Generation

Project Lead · Advised by Prof. Zhengzhe Liu

Dec 2025 – Apr 2026

Feedforward generation of 3D food models with interiors, so that cut-open views render realistic cross-sections for foods with varied exterior shapes. Distilling score distillation sampling (SDS) knowledge into a feedforward model generates an interior in under one second, instead of slow per-object optimization.

Real2Sim-Guided Model Predictive Control for Few-Shot Tabletop Manipulation

Research Intern · Advised by Prof. Gaurav S. Sukhatme

USC Robotic Embedded Systems Laboratory · Aug 2025 – Aug 2026

An MPC pipeline with a custom reward model for few-shot tabletop manipulation. Its Real2Sim stage combines VLMs, Grounded SAM, TRELLIS, and FoundationPose to reconstruct common tabletop tasks (Push-T, cube picking, peg insertion) and update object poses in real time; zeroth-order optimization recovers mass and friction from video with 70%+ accuracy.

Post-Training Optimization for High-Precision Isodose Line Prediction

Research Intern / Project Lead · Advised by Prof. Ruishan Liu

May 2025 – May 2026

Dose predictions from state-of-the-art models are not yet accurate enough at isodose lines for clinical use. Post-training methods for head-and-neck dose prediction improve HD95 on the V50%, V12Gy, and V80% isodose lines over the baseline.

LLM-Guided Machine-Learning-Ready Metabolic Knowledge Graph Construction

Research Intern · Advised by Prof. Zheng Chen

Zheng Research Group, Osaka University · May 2025 – Jul 2026

Combines LLMs with expert knowledge to build a machine-learning-friendly metabolism dataset linking more than 20,000 papers to their KEGG pathways and GEO results. Expert verification of data quality is underway; paper in progress.

Protein CLIP

Training CLIP with Small Batch Size: A Method to Leverage Memory-Heavy Structural Data

Zhangyu Wang

CSCI 467 Course Project · Spring 2025 / Paper (PDF) / Code & data

Brings memory-heavy 3D protein structures into CLIP-style contrastive learning, where they force small batch sizes. Residual Loss Incorporation and Random Row-wise CLIP (with sigmoid and combined objectives), built on a trainable GearNet structure encoder, lower the minimum validation loss from 4.8 to 3.8–4.3 compared with a pre-embedded ProteinCLIP baseline; downstream protein-protein interaction performance remains an open problem.

EEG / EMG Control

EEG/EMG Signal-Based Control

Project Lead · Advised by Prof. Zheng Chen

Zheng Research Group, Osaka University · Mar 2026 – Jun 2026

Uses the correlation between EEG and EMG signals during actions to extract subject-agnostic features for EEG/EMG-guided manipulation, applying a learnable wavelet transform to EMG and exploring filters for noisy EEG signals.
